  1. Normative ground

    Line up the Brief A posters by tool. What did each tool make easy, and what did it treat as a “good” poster? Who decided that?

  2. Appropriation

    Brief B: which tool let you break its rules, and what did you have to do? Did the friction help or get in the way?

  3. Vertical and horizontal movement

    After Repeat, Workbench’s copies can’t be edited as a set. Where else could you move down a level of abstraction but not back up? Move a poster between tools with Copy SVG. What survived?

  4. Shneiderman vs. Li et al.

    Rate each tool on low threshold, high ceiling, and wide walls. Could one tool score well on all three without imposing its own idea of good work?
